## ABOUT THE PROJECT
#One-Platform-Two-Missions.
The vision of One Platform, Two Missions is to create a community where technology makes it easier for people to help others. By bringing blood donation and food donation together on one platform, the project aims to transform individual acts of kindness into meaningful social impact.

One Platform, Two Missions is a social-impact platform designed to address two important community needs through a single digital solution: saving lives through blood donation and reducing hunger through food donation.
The platform connects people who are willing to donate with those who are in need. Users can choose between “Donate Blood” and “Donate Food”, making it easier for individuals to contribute to their community in a simple and organized way.
